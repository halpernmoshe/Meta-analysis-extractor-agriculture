## 5. Root Cause Analysis

### 5.1 Treatment Arm Ambiguity (Primary Cause, affects 7/10 elements)

Loladze 2014 selected the **EC-only arm** (CO2 elevated, O3 ambient) as the representative
elevated-CO2 observation. Our system extracted both EC and EC+EO arms, and the validation
best-match algorithm selected whichever candidate was closest to the GT lnRR.

For most macronutrients (N, K, Ca), the EC+EO arm produces a slightly smaller decline than
EC-only (because O3 stresses the plant, partially counteracting CO2 dilution), so the validation
algorithm selected EC+EO as closer to GT — but this is the wrong biological arm. The correct
arm (EC-only) would have been an even better match for N and K, but was slightly less close for Ca.

For **Mn** specifically, this creates a **direction reversal**: EC-only (+2.5%) vs EC+EO (-16.4%).
Loladze correctly recorded the EC-only result as a slight Mn increase. Our system's best candidate
(-10.9%) came from either EC+EO or a mix of EC and vision observations, giving the wrong sign.

when the paper has a factorial design with ozone, since
