## 1. Paper Design

**Species:** Rice (*Oryza sativa* L. cv. Jarrah), early-maturing Australian cultivar.

**CO2 levels:** 350 vs 700 µL CO2 L-1, maintained in controlled growth chambers.

**Phosphorus factorial:** 6 P addition levels (0, 30, 60, 120, 240, 480 mg CaHPO4·2H2O kg-1 soil). 10 pots per P level, 5 assigned to each CO2 chamber. n=5 replicates per cell.

**Growth conditions:** 6 kg soil per pot (8L, 40×8 cm); flooded; 12 h light at 28°C; 21°C dark; N added as urea.

**Harvests:**
- **First harvest (56 DAP, maximum tillering):** shoots separated into leaves (blades) and sheaths, frozen in liquid N, freeze-dried. Chemical analysis of N, P, Ca, Zn, starch in both fractions.
- **Final harvest (139-146 DAP, grain maturity):** 700 µL treatment harvested at 139 DAP, 350 µL at 146 DAP. Shoot separated into leaves, stems, panicles. Grain chemical analysis for N and P (Table 2). Grain Ca and Zn reported graphically in Figures 3b and 4a. Grain Fe reported graphically in Figure 4b.

**Key design complexity:** Full 2 × 6 factorial (CO2 × P level), two harvest times, two tissue types at first harvest (blades vs sheaths), multiple grain fractions at final harvest.

**Variance:** LSD (p ≤ 0.05) reported for Table 2 grain data (LSD = 1.0 g/kg for N, 0.3 g/kg for P). No variance reported in Table 1 (leaf data). Figures show standard errors as error bars but values are not tabulated.

**PDF quality:** Scanned document with image tables. Recon flagged: SCANNED, IMAGE-TABLES, VAR-UNCLEAR. Estimated difficulty: HARD.
