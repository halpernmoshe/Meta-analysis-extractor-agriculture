# Public source data

The variance-provenance screens in Supplementary Material S7 use two published comparator datasets. They are publicly available from the original publishers:

| Comparator dataset | Public download location | Material used for the screen |
|---|---|---|
| Li J et al. (2022), biostimulant yield effectiveness | [Frontiers article and supplementary files](https://doi.org/10.3389/fpls.2022.836702) | `Data_Sheet_2.XLSX`, including treatment and control means, standard deviations, and sample sizes. |
| Li X et al. (2024), global biochar dataset | [Scientific Data article and linked data record](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-023-02867-9) | The released biochar workbook, including treatment and control means and standard deviations. |

These public article/data pages are the authoritative download locations. The repository does not redistribute publisher-hosted copies of those files.
