# 032_Kanowski_2001 - Agent Extraction

## Paper Details
- **Title**: Effects of elevated CO2 on the foliar chemistry of seedlings of two rainforest trees from north-east Australia: Implications for folivorous marsupials
- **Authors**: John Kanowski
- **Journal**: Austral Ecology (2001) 26, 165-172
- **Species**: Alphitonia petriei (pioneer), Flindersia brayleyana (mid-successional)
- **CO2 levels**: Ambient 350 ppm, Elevated 790 ppm
- **Duration**: 60 days in glasshouse
- **Soil types**: Basalt (nutrient-rich) and Rhyolite (nutrient-poor)
- **Design**: Unreplicated chambers, n=5 seedlings per treatment x soil combination

## Data Source
All mineral/element data from **Figure 1** (bar charts with mean +/- SE). No tabular data in this paper. Values estimated from figures and calibrated against text-reported percentage changes.

## Key Text-Reported Percentage Changes (used to calibrate figure readings)
- N: -25% in Alphitonia (decline of 4.5 mg/g), -29% in Flindersia (decline of 5.9 mg/g)
- Na: -44% in both species
- P: -19 to -28% in Flindersia (CO2 p=0.004); NS in Alphitonia (p=0.91)
- K: significant decline in Flindersia (CO2 p=0.003); NS in Alphitonia (p=0.93)
- Ca: significant decline in Flindersia (CO2 p=0.001); NS in Alphitonia (p=0.60)

## Notes
- Figure 2 contains phenolics, condensed tannins, leaf thickness, and leaf toughness data -- these are NOT mineral/element concentrations and are excluded.
- No other mineral elements (Fe, Zn, Mn, Cu, Mg, S, B, C, Mo, Si) were measured or reported.
- The CO2 treatment was unreplicated (single chamber per CO2 level), so P-values should be interpreted cautiously.
- Values are read from bar charts and are approximate. Percentage changes were calibrated against text-stated values.
