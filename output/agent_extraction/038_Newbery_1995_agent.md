## Paper Details
- **Title**: Nitrogen, phosphorus and potassium uptake and demand in Agrostis capillaris: the influence of elevated CO2 and nutrient supply

- **Year**: 1995
- **Journal**: New Phytologist
- **Species**: Agrostis capillaris

## Notes
- CO2 significantly reduced %K (Table 2 ANOVA: P<0.01)
- CO2 had no significant effect on %P, %N, or %C (Table 2)
- K demand (86Rb uptake) was significantly increased by elevated CO2
- Factorial design: P (2,10,30 mg/l), N (5,20,50 mg/l), K (5,20,50 mg/l)
- Only 10 of 27 treatments were selected for shoot chemical analysis
