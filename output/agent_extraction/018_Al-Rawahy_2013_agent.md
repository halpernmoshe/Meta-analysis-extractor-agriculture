# 018_Al-Rawahy_2013 - Agent Extraction

## Paper Info
- **Title**: Effect of O3 and CO2 Levels on Growth, Biochemical and Nutrient Parameters of Alfalfa (Medicago sativa)
- **Species**: Medicago sativa (Alfalfa, cv. Victor)
- **Experiment**: Open Top Chamber (OTC), University of Swansea, UK
- **Design**: Factorial - O3 (40/80/120 ppb), CO2 (350/400/450 ppm), Combined, plus Control
- **Duration**: 60 days exposure (starting day 31 post-germination)
- **n**: 30 plants per chamber
- **Control**: Charcoal-filtered ambient air (single chamber)

## Data Source
- **Table 3**: Nutrient parameters (mg/g dry weight) - 7 elements (N, K, P, Ca, Mg, Zn, Fe)
- Only CO2-alone treatments extracted (relevant for CO2 enrichment meta-analysis)
- No additional nutrient data in figures

## Notes
- The paper uses "ppb" for CO2 but this is clearly ppm (350/400/450 ppb CO2 would be meaninglessly low; ambient CO2 is ~400 ppm).
- The text states: "Most of the nutrients increased uniformly under the influence of CO2 enriched environment except N and K that showed a marginal decrease." This is consistent with the extracted data.
- All CO2 treatment P-values for nutrients are NS (not significant).
- The paper also has O3-only and combined O3+CO2 treatment data in Table 3, but those are not extracted here as they are not pure CO2 effects.
- SE values available in Table 3 but not extracted (can be added if needed).
