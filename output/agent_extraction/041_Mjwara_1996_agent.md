# 041_Mjwara_1996 - Agent Extraction

## Paper Details
- **Title**: Photosynthesis, growth and nutrient changes in non-nodulated *Phaseolus vulgaris* grown under atmospheric and elevated carbon dioxide conditions
- **Authors**: Mjwara, J. M., Botha, C. E. J. and Radloff, S. E.
- **Year**: 1996
- **Journal**: Physiologia Plantarum 97: 754-763
- **Species**: *Phaseolus vulgaris* L. cv. Contender (common bean)
- **Experiment Type**: Growth chamber (Controlled Environment)
- **CO2 Levels**: 360 (ambient) vs 700 (elevated) umol mol-1
- **Duration**: 10-40 days after germination (DAG)
- **Replicates**: n=3 for element concentrations, n=9 for growth

## Data Source
All mineral/element concentration data is presented in **figures only** (no tabular data for elements):
- **Fig 7**: Leaf nitrogen content (mg g-1 DW), n=3
- **Fig 8**: Macronutrients - A: Calcium, B: Potassium, C: Phosphorus, D: Magnesium (mg g-1 DW), n=3
- **Fig 9**: Micronutrients - A: Iron, B: Manganese, C: Zinc, D: Copper (ug g-1 DW), n=3

Table 1 provides F-statistics (significance) for treatment effects but no numeric concentration values.

## Key Findings (from text)
- **N**: Significantly reduced under elevated CO2 from 20 DAG onward (P<0.0001)
- **Ca**: Significantly reduced (P<0.0010)
- **P**: Significantly reduced (P<0.0010)
- **K**: Significantly increased (P<0.0001)
- **Mg**: Noticeable decline but NOT significant overall (P=0.07)
- **Fe**: Significantly reduced (P<0.0001)
- **Zn**: Significantly reduced (P<0.0001)
- **Mn**: Significantly increased (P<0.0001)
- **Cu**: NOT significant (P=0.81)

## Notes
1. All values are **estimated from figures** - no tabular element data exists in this paper.
2. The paper shows **time-dependent reversal** for several elements (Ca, Fe, Zn): initially higher under elevated CO2 early in growth, then lower at maturity.
3. K and Mn are the exceptions - they **increase** consistently under elevated CO2.
4. Cu showed no significant overall treatment effect (P=0.81).
5. The N data at 10 DAG was not clearly readable from the figure and was omitted.
6. For meta-analysis purposes, the **40 DAG** (final harvest) values are likely most comparable to other studies.
7. The text notes: "Foliar concentration of both micro- and macronutrients grown under elevated CO2 [were] generally reduced" - but this is an overall statement; K and Mn are clear exceptions.
