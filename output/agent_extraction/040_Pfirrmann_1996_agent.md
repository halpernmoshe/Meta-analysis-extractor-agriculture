# 040_Pfirrmann_1996 - Data Extraction Summary

## Paper Details
- **Title**: Effects of elevated CO2, O3 and K deficiency on Norway spruce (Picea abies): nutrient supply, content and leaching
- **Authors**: Pfirrmann, Barnes, Steiner, Schramel, Busch, Kuchenhoff, Payer
- **Journal**: New Phytologist (1996), 134, 267-278
- **Species**: Picea abies (Norway spruce)
- **Experiment**: Phytotron chamber, factorial CO2 x O3 x K fertilization

## Experimental Design
- **CO2 levels**: 350 (ambient) vs 750 (elevated) umol mol-1
- **O3 levels**: 20 (low) vs 75 (high) nmol mol-1
- **Soil K**: +K (well fertilized) vs -K (potassium-deficient)
- **Clones**: Two (773 and 399), data averaged across clones
- **Duration**: Single growing season (April-October 1989)
- **Factorial**: 2 CO2 x 2 O3 x 2 Soil K = 8 treatment combinations

## Data Sources
- **Table 3**: Current-year (1989) needle nutrient composition - 11 elements
- **Table 4**: Previous-year (1988) needle nutrient composition - 11 elements
- **Table 1**: Soil data (not extracted - soil nutrients, not plant tissue)
- **Table 2**: Fine root nutrient composition (not extracted - root data, same factorial)
- **Figure 3**: Throughfall chemistry (K, Mg, Ca leaching) - qualitative/time-course, not extractable as single means

## Elements Extracted
C, N, K, Mg, Ca, S, P, Cu, Zn, Fe, Mn (11 elements)

## Units
- C, N, K, Mg, Ca, P: mg g-1 d.wt
- S, Cu, Zn, Fe, Mn: ug g-1 d.wt

## Observations Summary
- **Total observations**: 88 (11 elements x 4 factorial combinations x 2 needle years)
- Each observation compares CO2=750 (treatment) vs CO2=350 (control) at a fixed O3 x Soil K combination

## Key CO2 Effects (from ANOVA in paper)
- **K**: CO2 significantly reduced K and P in current-year needles (P < 0.05)
- **C**: CO2 effect on C significant in both needle years (P < 0.05 current, P(*) previous)
- **N**: CO2 effect on N significant (P(*)) in current-year needles
- **S, Zn**: K deficiency associated with 30% lower S and Zn concentrations
- **Cu, Mn**: O3 exposure decreased Mn and increased Cu in current-year needles

## Notes
- Data represent means of two clones (773 and 399)
- Table 4 Fe value for O3=75/CO2=350/+K appears as "615" in the PDF but is interpreted as 61.5 (consistent with other Fe values in the 50-86 range)
- This is a complex factorial design; CO2 effects vary substantially across O3 and K conditions
- No sample sizes (n) are provided per cell in the tables
