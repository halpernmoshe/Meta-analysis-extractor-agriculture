# 015_Pleijel_2009 - Agent Extraction

## Paper
**Title:** Yield dilution of grain Zn in wheat grown in open-top chamber experiments with elevated CO2 and O3 exposure
**Authors:** Pleijel & Danielsson (2009), Journal of Cereal Science 50:278-282
**Species:** Triticum aestivum (wheat), cultivar Dragon (Exp 1-5) and Lantvete (Exp 6)
**Experiment type:** Open-top chamber (OTC), 6 experiments in SW Sweden

## Data Source
All data from **Table 1** - Zn concentration in wheat grain (mg/kg).
Only element reported is **Zn**. No other mineral concentrations are provided in the paper.
Figures show relative treatment effects (unitless ratios), not raw concentration data.

## Extraction Notes
- This paper has a factorial design: CO2 x O3 (Exp 1) and CO2 x irrigation (Exp 3)
- Each factorial combination extracted as separate observation
- Control = OTC control (ambient CO2) at same O3/irrigation level
- Experiments 4-6 are O3-only experiments (no elevated CO2) - NOT extracted for CO2 meta-analysis
- Only Zn is reported; no other elements measured

## Summary Statistics
- **Mean effect:** -9.45% (all 8 observations)
- **Range:** -18.42% to -3.23%
- **All effects negative** (CO2 reduces grain Zn concentration)
- Consistent with yield dilution hypothesis
