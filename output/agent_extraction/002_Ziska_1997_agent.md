# 002_Ziska_1997 - Agent Extraction

## Paper Details
- **Title**: Growth and Yield Response of Field-Grown Tropical Rice to Increasing Carbon Dioxide and Air Temperature
- **Authors**: Ziska, L.H., Namuco, O., Moya, T., Quilang, J.
- **Journal**: Agronomy Journal, 89:45-53 (1997)
- **Species**: Oryza sativa L. (rice, cv. IR 72)
- **Experiment**: Open-top field chambers, irrigated paddy, IRRI Philippines
- **Seasons**: 1994 wet (Aug-Nov), 1995 dry (Jan-Apr)
- **CO2 levels**: Ambient (~367 uL/L), +200 (~568 uL/L), +300 (~665 uL/L)
- **Temperature levels**: Ambient (~25.6C), Ambient+4C (~29.5C)
- **Design**: 2x3 factorial (2 temp x 3 CO2), 3 reps, RBD
- **n**: 3 replicate chambers per treatment

## Data Source: Table 4 (Grain Quality Parameters)
Table 4 reports CO2 effects averaged over both temperature treatments, and temperature effects averaged over all CO2 treatments. No interactive effects between CO2 and temperature were observed for grain quality.

**Note**: Ca and K were only measured in the 1994 wet season. Amylose was measured but is not a mineral element (excluded). No other mineral/element concentrations (e.g., Fe, Zn, Mn, Cu, Mg, N, P, S, etc.) were reported in this paper.

## Summary
- **Total observations**: 8
- **Elements**: Ca (2 obs), K (2 obs), Protein (4 obs)
- **Key finding**: Protein content decreased significantly at +300 CO2 in both seasons (-7.0% wet, -9.7% dry). Ca and K showed non-significant trends of decrease.
- **No other minerals reported**: This paper focused primarily on growth/yield responses; mineral analysis was limited to Ca, K, amylose, and protein in grain.
