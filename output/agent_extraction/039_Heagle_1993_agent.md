# Extraction: Heagle et al. (1993)

## Paper Details
- **Title**: Effects of ozone and carbon dioxide mixtures on two clones of white clover
- **Authors**: Allen S. Heagle, Joseph E. Miller, Dorothy E. Sherrill, John O. Rawlings
- **Journal**: New Phytologist (1993), 123, 751-762
- **Species**: *Trifolium repens* L. (white clover)
- **Clones**: NC-S (O3-sensitive) and NC-R (O3-resistant)

## Experimental Design
- **Type**: Factorial CO2 x O3 x Clone in greenhouse CSTRs (continuously stirred tank reactors)
- **CO2 levels**: 380 (ambient), 490, 600, 710 ul/l
- **O3 levels**: 5 nl/l (charcoal-filtered, CF) and 82 nl/l (O3-added)
- **Exposure**: 8 weeks, 24 h/d for CO2, 6 h/d for O3
- **Replicates**: 2 (experiment repeated), 8 chambers total
- **n per treatment**: ~4 plants (mean of samples per treatment)

## Data Source
- **Table 3**: Foliar nutrient concentrations after 8 weeks exposure (whole-canopy leaf analyses)
- 11 elements measured: N, P, K, S, Cu, B, Fe, Mn, Zn, Ca, Mg

## Extraction Summary
- **Total observations**: 132
- **Elements**: 11 (N, P, K, S, Cu, B, Fe, Mn, Zn, Ca, Mg) x 12 comparisons each
- **Comparisons**: 3 elevated CO2 levels x 2 O3 levels x 2 clones = 12 per element
- **Control**: 380 ul/l CO2 within each O3 x clone combination

## Key Findings (from paper)
- CO2 enrichment **decreased** foliar concentrations of N, P, K, S, Cu, B, and Fe
- CO2 enrichment **increased** foliar concentrations of Mn
- Zn, Ca, and Mg were **not significantly affected** by CO2
- O3 exposure did **not** modify the CO2 effects on foliar nutrients
- Two groups identified by PCA:
  - Group 1 (decreased by CO2): N, P, K, S, Cu, B, Fe
  - Group 2 (increased or unaffected): Mn, Zn, Ca, Mg

## Notes
- Factorial design: each CO2 x O3 x clone combination extracted as separate observation
- Control is always 380 ul/l CO2 within the same O3 level and clone
- The paper notes dilution from increased growth explains decreases in N, P, K, S, Cu, B, Fe
- Mn increase is notable and runs counter to dilution hypothesis
- Treatment numbers in Table 3 correspond to: 1-4 (NC-S, CF), 5-8 (NC-R, CF), 9-12 (NC-S, O3), 13-16 (NC-R, O3)
