# 012_Wu_2004 - Agent Extraction

## Paper Details
- **Title**: Effects of elevated CO2 concentration on growth, water use, yield and grain quality of wheat under two soil water levels
- **Authors**: Dong-Xiu Wu, Gen-Xuan Wang, Yong-Fei Bai, Jian-Xiong Liao
- **Journal**: Agriculture, Ecosystems and Environment 104 (2004) 493-507
- **Species**: *Triticum aestivum* L. cv. Ganmai 8139 (spring wheat)

## Experimental Design
- **Type**: Growth chamber, factorial design (2 CO2 x 2 soil moisture)
- **CO2 levels**: Ambient (350 uL/L) vs Elevated (700 uL/L)
- **Soil moisture**: High (80% FWC) vs Low (40% FWC)
- **Treatments**: HC (ambient+high moisture), HD (elevated+high moisture), LC (ambient+low moisture), LD (elevated+low moisture)
- **Replicates**: 6 pots per treatment; 3 replicates for chemical analysis

## Mineral/Element Data (Table 1 - 80% FWC only)

Table 1 reports grain nutrient concentrations only for the favorable soil water condition (80% FWC): HC vs HD.

| Element | Tissue | Control (HC) | Treatment (HD) | Effect (%) | Unit | Significance |
|---------|--------|-------------|---------------|-----------|------|-------------|
| N       | grain  | 3.34 +/- 0.018 | 2.83 +/- 0.014 | -15.27% | g/100g | ** |
| P       | grain  | 0.658 +/- 0.040 | 0.417 +/- 0.004 | -36.63% | g/100g | ** |
| K       | grain  | 0.625 +/- 0.021 | 0.480 +/- 0.042 | -23.20% | g/100g | ** |
| Zn      | grain  | 56.03 +/- 1.26 | 37.75 +/- 0.63 | -32.61% | mg/kg | ** |

### Non-mineral quality parameters (Table 1, for reference only)

## Table 2 - Per Hectare Nutritive Values (NOT concentrations - excluded from extraction)
Table 2 reports total nutrient yield per hectare (kg/ha or g/ha), which reflects both concentration changes and yield increases. These are NOT concentration data and are excluded from the mineral concentration extraction. All per-hectare values increased under elevated CO2 due to large yield gains offsetting concentration decreases.

## Notes
- The paper uses a factorial design (CO2 x soil moisture) but Table 1 only reports concentration data for the 80% FWC condition
- No separate concentration values are given for the 40% FWC (low moisture) condition
- The text confirms all four elements (N, P, K, Zn) decreased under elevated CO2
- All concentration decreases were statistically significant (** = P < 0.01) except lysine (NS at P=0.06)
- Crude protein = N x 5.7 (so protein and N changes are identical at -15.2%)
- Figures 1-5 contain growth, yield, water use data but no additional mineral concentration data
