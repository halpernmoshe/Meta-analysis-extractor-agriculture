# 058_ONeill_1987 - Agent Extraction

## Paper Details
- **Title**: Elevated atmospheric CO2 effects on seedling growth, nutrient uptake, and rhizosphere bacterial populations of Liriodendron tulipifera L.
- **Authors**: O'Neill, E.G., Luxmoore, R.J., Norby, R.J.
- **Year**: 1987
- **Journal**: Plant and Soil 104, 3-11
- **Species**: Liriodendron tulipifera (yellow-poplar)
- **CO2 levels**: Ambient = 367 ul/l, Elevated = 692 ul/l
- **Duration**: 24 weeks in controlled environment chambers
- **Replicates**: n = 6 per treatment
- **Soil**: Unfertilized, nutrient-poor forest soil (Typic Dystrocrept)

## Data Source
All concentration data from **Table 2** (nutrient concentration section, mg/g dry mass). The paper reports whole-plant nutrient concentrations, not tissue-specific values.

## Extraction Notes
- The paper categorizes elements into three groups based on response pattern:
  - **Category I** (N, S, B): Concentration significantly lower at elevated CO2; total content unchanged. Growth increased without additional uptake.
  - **Category II** (P, K, Cu, Al, Fe): Concentration not significantly different; total content higher. Uptake proportional to growth.
  - **Category III** (Ca, Mg, Sr, Ba, Zn, Mn): No significant differences in concentration or total content.
- All elements showed negative effect_pct (concentration decline), consistent with CO2-induced dilution.
- Note: Table 2 header says "642 ul/l" but this appears to be a typo; the text consistently says 692 ul/l.
