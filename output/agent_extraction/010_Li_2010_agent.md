# 010_Li_2010 (actually Högy et al. 2009) - Agent Extraction

## Paper Details
- **Title**: Effects of elevated CO2 on grain yield and quality of wheat: results from a 3-year free-air CO2 enrichment experiment
- **Authors**: Högy, Wieser, Köhler, Schwadorf, Breuer, Franzaring, Muntifering, Fangmeier
- **Year**: 2009
- **Journal**: Plant Biology 11 (Suppl. 1), 60-69
- **Species**: Triticum aestivum L. cv. TRISO (spring wheat)
- **Experiment**: FACE (Free-Air CO2 Enrichment), 3 years (2004-2006)
- **CO2 levels**: Ambient ~400 ppm vs Elevated ~550 ppm (+150 ppm enrichment)
- **Replicates**: 5 per treatment per year, data pooled across 3 years

## Important Notes
- This PDF is mislabeled as "010_Li_2010" but is actually Högy et al. 2009.
- **No tables with absolute mean values for minerals.** All mineral data presented as relative % change in Figure 2 (bar chart).
- Absolute protein values stated in Discussion: AMBIENT=15.5%, FACE=14.2%.
- N derived from protein using conversion factor 5.7.
- Al and B % changes explicitly stated in text (-11.7% and -26.3%).

## Summary
- **21 observations** extracted (N, C, S, P, K, Ca, Mg, Na, Fe, Zn, Cu, Mn, Se, Mo, Cr, Ni, Si, B, Al, Cd, Pb)

- Significant increases: K (+18%), Pb (+30%)
- Significant decreases: Fe (-10%), Cd (-15%), N/protein (-7.4/-8.4%)
- Trends: Mo increase, Mg and Si decrease
