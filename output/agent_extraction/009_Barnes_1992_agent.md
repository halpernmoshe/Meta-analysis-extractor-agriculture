# 009_Barnes_1992 - Agent Extraction

## Paper Details
- **Title**: The influence of CO2 and O3, singly and in combination, on gas exchange, growth and nutrient status of radish (Raphanus sativus L.)
- **Authors**: Barnes, J.D. and Pfirrmann, T.
- **Year**: 1992
- **Journal**: New Phytologist, 121, 403-412
- **Species**: Raphanus sativus L. cv. Cherry Belle (radish)

## Experimental Design
- **Type**: Factorial CO2 x O3 in controlled phytotron chambers
- **CO2 levels**: Ambient ~385 umol/mol vs Elevated ~765 umol/mol
- **O3 levels**: Non-polluted ~20 nmol/mol vs Polluted ~80 nmol/mol
- **Duration**: 27 days after transfer (32 days total from emergence)
- **Replication**: 4 plants per chamber, 2 chambers per treatment (n=8)
- **Harvest**: Whole plant, separated into shoot and root (+hypocotyl)
- **Analysis**: N, C by Carlo Erba analyser; P, S, K, Ca, Mg by ICP-AES

## Data Sources
- **Table 4**: Shoot macronutrient composition (% shoot dry weight)
- **Table 5**: Root (+hypocotyl) macronutrient composition (% root dry weight)
- No additional mineral data in figures (Figures 1-3 show gas exchange data only)
- C/N ratio reported but C not reported separately

## Summary Statistics
- **Total observations**: 24 (6 elements x 2 tissues x 2 O3 levels)
- **Elements**: N, S, P, K, Mg, Ca
- **Mean effect across all observations**: -12.70%
- **Most negative**: S in root at low O3 (-32.61%)
- **Most positive**: Ca in shoot at low O3 (+2.78%)

## Key Patterns
- CO2 enrichment consistently reduced N, S, P, Mg concentrations in both tissues
- Root effects generally larger than shoot effects (especially for S, Mg, Ca)
- K showed small/mixed effects in shoot but consistent decline in root
- Ca increased slightly in shoot at low O3 but declined in root
- O3 moderated CO2 effects: polluted O3 generally reduced the CO2-induced decline (CO2 x O3 interaction counteracted)
- Non-polluted air showed stronger CO2 effects than polluted air for most elements in shoot

## Notes
- C/N ratio was reported (Table 4: 9.41, 10.09, 11.28, 9.90; Table 5: 26.85, 24.79, 32.69, 26.72) but C concentration alone was not tabulated
- No micronutrients (Fe, Zn, Mn, Cu, B, etc.) were measured
- Values with letter suffixes indicate LSD groupings at 1% level
