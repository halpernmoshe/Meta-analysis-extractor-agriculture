## Summary
- **15 total observations** extracted from Table 3 (5 elements x 3 time points)
- **Elements**: C, N, P, Ca, Fe
- **Patterns**:
  - N strongly decreased (-27 to -33%) under elevated CO2
  - C strongly increased (+21 to +34%)
  - P moderately increased (+8 to +12%)
  - Ca and Fe showed small, non-significant decreases (-1 to -6%)
