# 006_Azam_2013 - Agent Extraction

## Paper Info
- **Title**: Yield, chemical composition and nutritional quality responses of carrot, radish and turnip to elevated atmospheric carbon dioxide
- **Authors**: Azam, Khan, Mahmood, Hameed (2013)
- **Journal**: J Sci Food Agric 93: 3237-3244
- **Species**: Carrot (Daucus carota cv. T-1-111), Radish (Raphanus sativus cv. Mino), Turnip (Brassica rapa cv. Grabe)
- **CO2 levels**: Ambient 400 umol/mol vs Elevated 1000 umol/mol
- **Experiment**: Greenhouse, potted plants, triplicate
- **Tissue**: Root tuber (dry weight basis)

## Data Source
All elemental data from **Table 3** (Elemental composition).

Note: Table 3 for Radish only reports Ca, Mg, K (macro) and Zn, Mn, Fe, Pb, Ni, Cu, Cr (micro). C, N, H, S are not reported for radish.

Note: Pb, Ni, Cr, Cd are heavy metals/trace elements not typically included in Loladze meta-analysis and are excluded from the extraction below.
